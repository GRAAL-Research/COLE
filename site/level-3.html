Lost in Translation: Why SOTA LLMs Struggle with French NLU Frontiers

David Beauchemin, Yan Tremblay, Mohamed Youssef, Richard Khoury (2026)

ICLR 2026, Poster in Workshop: I Can't Believe It's Not Better: Where Large Language Models need to improve. This is a workshop poster, not a main-conference paper.C’est une affiche d’atelier, pas un article de la conférence principale.

Read the accepted paperLire l’article accepté

Abstract (English original)Résumé (original anglais)

Despite LLMs' dominance in English, their transferability to French NLU remains inconsistent. To characterize this limitation, we present COLE, a new benchmark comprising 23 diverse French tasks, and use it to reveal significant failure modes in state-of-the-art (SOTA) models. Our analysis reveals three critical negative results: 1) a persistent performance gap where top open-weight models lag behind closed models by over 20%, 2) the illusion of specialization, where surface-level fluency in tuned models masks deep reasoning deficits, and 3) catastrophic failure in zero-shot extractive QA and regional dialect understanding, where many models, including top-tier reasoning models, achieve 0% Exact Match or perform near random baselines. We analyze these unexpected failures to highlight specific frontiers (morphology, cultural nuance) where scaling laws currently fail to generalize.

Protocol noteNote de protocole

A task score is the mean of its metrics among accuracy, f1, pearson, pearsonr, spearman, fquad and exact match. A value above 1 is divided by 100.Le score d’une tâche est la moyenne de ses métriques parmi accuracy, f1, pearson, pearsonr, spearman, fquad et exact match. Une valeur supérieure à 1 est divisée par 100.

A skill score is the mean of the tasks the model has. Missing tasks are never counted as zero. The overall score follows the live leaderboard.Le score d’une compétence est la moyenne des tâches du modèle. Une tâche manquante n’est jamais comptée comme zéro. Le score général suit le classement en ligne.

To check with the authors: the entry frblimp duplicates qfrblimp in all 96 results, so it is left out and counted once; in this snapshot the Quebec grammar test (qfrblimp) therefore cannot be told apart from the general one. The wsd metric also holds values below 1.À vérifier avec les auteurs : l’entrée frblimp duplique qfrblimp dans les 96 résultats ; elle est donc écartée et comptée une fois. Dans ce relevé, le test de grammaire québécoise (qfrblimp) ne se distingue donc pas de la version générale. La métrique wsd contient aussi des valeurs inférieures à 1.
